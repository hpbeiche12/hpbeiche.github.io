KeystoneCue

AI portfolio intelligence for property investors and managers.

KeystoneCue consolidates listings, valuations, rent rolls, expenses, and market signals into a single live view of a property portfolio. An AI engine forecasts cash flow, flags under-performing assets, estimates fair-market value drift, and surfaces the next best action — buy, hold, refinance, or sell.

  • Automated valuation & rent-comparison models
  • AI cash-flow and ROI forecasting per property
  • Document ingestion (leases, statements) with smart extraction
  • Portfolio dashboards, alerts, and what-if scenarios
  • Machine Learning
  • Predictive Analytics
  • Document AI
  • Cloud-native
AI · FinTech
PECUNA and PECUNA-Mobile

PECUNA and PECUNA-Mobile

AI financial tracking & forecasting for growing businesses.

PECUNA unifies bank feeds, invoices, and expenses into a real-time financial command center. Its AI categorizes transactions, detects anomalies and duplicate spend, projects cash position weeks ahead, and explains variance in plain language — so finance teams spend less time reconciling and more time deciding.

  • Automated transaction categorization & reconciliation
  • Cash-flow forecasting with confidence bands
  • Anomaly & fraud-signal detection
  • Natural-language variance and trend explanations
  • LLM Integration
  • Time-series Forecasting
  • Anomaly Detection
  • Secure APIs
